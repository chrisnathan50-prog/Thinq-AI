Questions, Answered

Can't find what you need? Our support team replies within a few hours.

Contact support
What counts as a task?

Anything Thinq finishes for you: a drafted reply, a booked meeting, a summary, a report. Reading your inbox or planning your day doesn't count.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No. You get 14 days of Pro for free. At the end you can pick a plan or carry on with Starter.

Can Thinq send emails without asking me?

Not unless you turn that on. By default, anything that leaves your account waits for your approval.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Your data is only used to do work for you, and it's never used to train shared models. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, anytime from your settings. Upgrades start right away; downgrades start at your next billing date.
