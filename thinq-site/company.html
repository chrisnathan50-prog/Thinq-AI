About Thinq

We're Building the Assistant
We Always Wanted.

Thinq started in 2023 with a simple frustration: smart people spending half their week on work a computer could do. We're a team of 38 across London, Lisbon and Toronto, fixing that.

Our job is to hand people back the hours that busywork takes — so they can spend them on what they're best at.

What We Believe

Three ideas that shape every feature we ship.

Calm over clever

A good assistant is quiet. It does the work and stays out of your way.

Trust is earned

Thinq asks before it acts. You can see, change and undo everything it does.

Built for real days

We design for messy inboxes and moved meetings, not perfect demos.
