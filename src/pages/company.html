About Thinq

We're Building the Assistant
We Always Wanted.

Thinq started in 2023 with a simple frustration: smart people spending half their week on work a computer could do. We're a team of 38 across London, Lisbon and Toronto, fixing that.

Our job is to hand people back the hours that busywork takes — so they can spend them on what they're best at.
